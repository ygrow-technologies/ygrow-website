\# Career Analytics



\## Turn Your Job Search Into a Measurable Process



Most developers don't know exactly where their career search is breaking down.



YGrow can help visualize the career funnel.



\### Example Funnel



\*\*Opportunities\*\*



↓  



\*\*Applications\*\*



↓  



\*\*Recruiter Conversations\*\*



↓  



\*\*Technical Interviews\*\*



↓  



\*\*Final Interviews\*\*



↓  



\*\*Offers\*\*



The numbers can tell a story.



For example:



\- Many applications but few recruiter responses may indicate a positioning problem.

\- Strong recruiter conversion but weak technical conversion may indicate interview preparation issues.

\- Strong interview performance but few offers may indicate opportunity or company-fit issues.



The goal is not to turn career growth into a collection of numbers.



The goal is to make the process easier to understand.
